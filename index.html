Capabilities

One studio holds the chain from concept development through engineering, mold making, casting, finishing, coordination, and installation.

  • Metal castingSand casting, lost wax, lost PLA, resin bonded sand, match plate patterns. Bronze, aluminum, iron.
  • GlassKiln cast glass, lead crystal, slumped and fused glass, cold working and polishing, architectural lamination with partners.
  • FinishesHot and cold patina, plating and anodizing coordination, custom color development, outdoor rated coatings.
  • Digital fabricationParametric modeling in Grasshopper, 3D scanning and reverse engineering, CNC routing and machining, in house 3D printing.
  • Metal fabricationWelded and machined assemblies, architectural stairs and screens, structural coordination with engineers of record.
  • RestorationDocumentation of historic profiles, reproduction of missing ornament, code and ADA upgrades that keep period character.
  • Project managementBudgeting, prototyping, mockups and samples, shop drawings, permitting support, sequencing, and site installation.

Studio

ChiLab Studio is a Chicago-based project development and fabrication studio working across art, design, architecture, and public space.

Founded in 2013, ChiLab Studio was built around a collaborative approach to making, bringing together art, design, engineering, and fabrication under one roof. The studio works with artists, designers, architects, and institutions to develop and realize ambitious projects, particularly those that demand close attention to material, process, detail, and execution.
