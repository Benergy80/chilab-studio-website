Custom lighting, metalwork, furnishings, and artistic details for the Fulton Market location.

The work

Custom lighting, metalwork, furnishings, and artistic details for Kuma's Corner at Peoria and Fulton Market. Kuma's has a real culture, and the point was to keep it authentic and at the forefront rather than contrive a theme.

The material question

A franchise opening new locations needs coherent signature elements. The risk is that coherence turns into a theme restaurant. The honesty at Kuma's is the people who work there, so their hands had to be in the room.

The making

Employees contributed their own illustrations and panels, which we built into the bar itself. With one of the cooks, an incredible filigree illustrator, we developed stained glass windows from his drawings, made with our friend Steven Krejcik. Hand blown Boolean pendants circle the bar, with larger ones in the west wall portals, and below bar lights and a stage truss for the hanging bar shelves give the location its signature look.

In place

The coherent pieces carry from location to location. What makes it Kuma's is the culture itself, and the layers the people working there added over the top of it.
