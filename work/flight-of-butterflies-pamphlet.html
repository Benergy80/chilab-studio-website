ChiLab Studio
Flight of Butterflies
Public Art

Twenty-nine cast aluminum butterfly sculptures installed across Chicago, each finished by a local artist.

The work

Twenty-nine six foot butterflies across Chicago, from Lakeview to South Shore to Austin, each finished by a different local artist, for the Peggy Notebaert Nature Museum. Two species, the Regal Fritillary and the Black Swallowtail, carried out of the museum and into neighborhoods across the city.

The material question

One blank, twenty-nine artists, and a year outdoors in Chicago. The sculpture had to be a clean canvas any artist could work on, and survive whatever they put on it.

The making

Bodies modeled in both motifs, rendered into molds, and cast in aluminum on steel bases, every casting hand finished to take paint. Then the studio itself changed: we reconfigured the whole shop and opened a new section so the artists could work on site, and spent most of those months getting them information, materials, and room. Where we had concerns about an artist's materials we raised them through Cortney Lederer of CNL Projects, curator and liaison. Mostly the choice was deliberate, and the artist was right.

In place

Most pieces held up and surprised us. A few showed nature overtaking a medium, where an artist had stepped away from what we recommended. In Lederer's words: “Twenty-nine artists participated and all required different logistical needs and material expertise, and ChiLab delivered.”
