Glass  /  2023

Jail Debris

Maria Gaspar

Twenty three cast lead crystal bars and bricks taken from a demolished Chicago corrections facility.

Rubber molds were taken from bars and bricks salvaged from a demolished Chicago corrections facility, then cast in lead crystal and polished on selected faces. Twenty three elements in total, produced for artist Maria Gaspar.
