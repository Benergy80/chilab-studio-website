Chase Tower Executive Floors

JPMorgan Chase corporate executive offices, floors 56 and 57

Dark patinated, bronze finished steel portals, elevator surrounds, shelving and panels for Chase's corporate executive offices at Chase Tower, in the finish developed for the Litowitz Family Office.

Dark patinated, bronze finished steel portals, elevator surrounds and jamb liners, library and shelving metal for Chase Bank's corporate executive offices on floors 56 and 57 of Chase Tower in Chicago. Designed by Gensler Chicago, with ECI as general contractor; Multimetal Products fabricated the steel, Parenti & Raffaelli installed it, and ChiLab carried out the patina and finish developed for the Litowitz Family Office.
