Parametric Feature Wall
Architectural

A cast metal wall derived from the bird's eye view of a shipping yard, modeled parametrically and cast component by component.

The work

The idea came from the team at Gensler Chicago: a freight yard seen from above, containers stacked at varied heights, abstracted into a wall. It ties what Uber Freight does to the room where it does it.

The material question

A field of unique parts. Every panel different, all of them reading as one continuous surface, and all of them draft free, which conventional patterns cannot give you.

The making

We built a parametric model in Grasshopper tuned to the space, sliced it into individual cast components, and printed the sand molds straight from the digital files, with no pattern in between. Done conventionally it would easily have cost four times as much and still could not have been draft free; the design was only possible because that technology had just arrived. It was incredibly efficient with our electric furnace and we went from 3D model to a cast metal wall relief in just 3 weeks.

In place

The castings were finished and assembled on site into a single continuous surface precisely encapsulating the digital screen, one of two pieces we built for the building.
