St. Alfred

Chicago flagship retail fixtures

A powder coated steel fixture system and stone topped cash counter for the Chicago streetwear boutique.

A custom retail fixture and display package for St. Alfred, produced with Chelsea Lombardo of Interiors + Objects.

We translated the retail design into a single powder coated steel system: display fixtures, modified industrial shelving, shoe racks, hanger bars, and a modular center display, with a steel clad cash counter topped in stone.
