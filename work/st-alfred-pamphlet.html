St. Alfred
Retail

A powder coated steel fixture system and stone topped cash counter for the Chicago streetwear boutique.

The work

St. Alfred is a Chicago streetwear boutique. For its flagship, Chelsea Lombardo designed a store that would read as one room: every fixture part of the same object rather than a collection of furniture.

The material question

One language across everything. Custom pieces and bought ones had to become indistinguishable, so the whole room would read as a single, specific monochrome.

The making

We translated the retail design into shop drawings and a single steel fixture system: display fixtures, modified industrial shelving, custom shoe racks, hanger bars, and a modular center display, with a steel clad cash counter topped in stone as the anchor of the floor. Everything we fabricated was powder coated, and the parts we bought, down to the shelving used for the clothing racks, were sent out for the same powder coat.

In place

Coordinated around an active construction schedule and assembled on site. A fantastic collaboration with Chelsea Lombardo, with lighting designed and built by Max Davis.
