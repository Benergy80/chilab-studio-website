Cast glass logo displays engineered for the brand's first brick and mortar storefronts.

The work

For SKIMS's first brick and mortar stores, Willo Perron of Perron-Roettinger Studio wanted the logo to emerge from the storefront glass. The first premise followed the store's fabrics: soft, voluminous body forms pushing out of the window.

The material question

Soft forms projecting from glass are hard to make and could not hold the fidelity of the logo. So the question became whether thick glass castings could be laminated to oversized architectural windows. As far as anyone knew, it had not been done.

The making

We cast the logo; AGNORA, North America's large format glass specialist, laminated it to the storefront glass. Both sides had a good feeling about it, and until the prototype came through the process we were all holding our breath. We saw it at AGNORA's facility in Canada, beside the largest autoclave we had ever seen and a 32 foot conference table made from a single sheet of glass.

In place

The castings read as prismatic lenses. Walking up, you see into the store as if through a galaxy. A first of its kind, in Miami, Atlanta, and Houston.
