Palimpsest

Industry of the Ordinary, Driehaus Museum

A Driehaus Museum doorknob, 3D scanned and recast in clay taken from the Chicago River.

For the exhibition Materialities at the Driehaus Museum, Industry of the Ordinary remade a brass doorknob from the door between the family rooms and the servants' quarters in clay taken from the Chicago River, the same clay used for the bricks that rebuilt the mansion after the Great Chicago Fire.

We 3D scanned the original in place, modeled it, made molds, and pressed and fired the clay duplicate.
