Chrome tubes rising from the sixth floor to the seventh floor skylight, with the KNOLL name in dark bronze.

The work

The marquee for Knoll's Fulton Market flagship rises from the sixth floor to the skylight on the seventh, a row of chrome tubes with the KNOLL name in dark bronze. Its repeating wireframe is a conscious quote of Harry Bertoia, whose work for Knoll in the 1950s still sets the company's design language, and it was a reference shared between us and the architects at Gensler. Getting to quote that history was an honor.

The material question

Knoll's architects first proposed black pipe. We proposed chrome plated steel, set against the warm red marble of the entry so the piece reads as part of the room rather than an object placed in it. Knoll's directors latched onto it.

The making

Developed through samples in both steel and bronze before approval: roughly 150 custom components and 300 cut and welded caps, built on a fast track of four to five weeks.

In place

The flagship opened in June 2019 and was later featured in Interior Design. For us the marquee is a standalone work of art we got to design and build.

Also at Fulton Market

Alongside the marquee we worked with the designers of Knoll's Antenna table and their manufacturing studio on a 24 foot solid bronze recreation of the table, and made steel handrail covers for the showroom. That work was a collaboration inside an established design. The marquee was ours.
