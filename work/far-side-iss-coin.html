Far Side ISS Commemorative Coin

Cast silver coins for a Space Force research mission

An edition of eight hand cast .999 silver coins carrying a Gary Larson Far Side image to the International Space Station.

An edition of eight cast silver coins made for a U.S. Air Force Academy research mission, honoring Gary Larson by flying his Far Side image to the International Space Station.

We translated Larson's drawing into 3D relief, printed each coin in castable resin, and cast it by lost wax in .999 silver, about 1.5 inches across. The coins launched aboard the Polaris mission on May 15, 2026.
