Dark patinated, bronze finished steel and reeded glass partitions, shelving, and doors across a family office floor, built and installed with union crews.

The work

A sixth floor build out for the Litowitz Family Office at 1616 Orrington in Evanston: demountable steel and glass partitions, boardroom and elevator entry assemblies, library and lounge metalwork, back bar and column shelving, and custom doors, all in one dark bronze steel and reeded glass language from the elevator lobby through the boardrooms to the lounge. The direction came from the client and Gensler together, with the Gensler team, as so often, the creative engine behind it.

The material question

Reeded glass is extremely difficult to work with. Every piece was valuable, so every measurement had to be right. The doors were big and heavy enough to demand acute attention to detail. And this was never one wall: it was a system for hanging and mounting walls, which had to land on the millwork, the soffits, and the building's pipes without a misaligned line.

The making

We surveyed the existing conditions precisely enough that no shim or leveling gap ever had to be too wide, coordinated hardware with custom built doors, and developed methods for the curtain wall mullions and for installing them safely. Where a pipe could not move we removed a vertical mullion, and we extended a soffit to keep the geometry symmetrical from the lobby through the lounge. The steel was patinated dark, then given a bronze finish, a finish that grew out of our cast metal walls. We fabricated the system and directed Caputo's union installers, a stunning team.

In place

It was our largest architectural package, and we learned a great deal from it. What we are proudest of is how beautiful the room turned out. Seeing the artwork that now hangs there, in its company, we are acutely aware of how special a place it is. The patina has since opened an entirely new market for the studio: we used the same finish again on the executive floors of Chase Bank Tower in downtown Chicago.
