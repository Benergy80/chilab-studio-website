ChiLab Studio began as a collaboration between friends from different disciplines. Driven by a mutual interest in giving everyday objects and public experiences more care, the ChiLab think tank has grown into an award winning specialty project studio and a working part of the Chicago design and arts communities.

We bring artists' and designers' visions to life through bespoke project development and artisanal fabrication. Our team works closely with clients to understand what they are after, providing guidance and support through the whole process, and we partner with a network of specialists and industrial manufacturers so that every part of a project holds to the same standard.

True to the beginning, we welcome collaboration and take on commissions on a per project basis. Whether a client needs fine art fabrication, custom furniture and accessories, artisanal finishes and details, or project management support, we have the expertise and the shop to make it happen.

The studio was founded in 2013 by Ben Stagl. Ally Reza is partner, co-owner, and co-director of ChiLab, and leads the studio's glass practice, which runs from lead crystal casting to large format architectural glass. Ben holds an MFA in Sculpture from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago and a BFA in Sculpture from Oregon State University, and teaches in SAIC's Architecture, Interior Architecture, and Designed Objects program.
